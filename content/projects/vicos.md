A little desktop pastiche in the style of Windows 3.1/95, inspired by [Teethy.ca](https://teethy.ca). Draggable windows, a working taskbar clock, a snow effect in the winter months, and an 8-bit rendition of Luma from Mario Galaxy playing in the background.

Originally lived at `/vicos` on this site, now split out into its own repo with its own commit history. It's a plain static site with no build step.
