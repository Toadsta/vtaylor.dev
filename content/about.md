Outside the classroom I still build things: a macOS menu bar app for switching DNS providers, this site's blog and projects system, and a retro Windows 95 style desktop for the browser.

When I'm not teaching or coding you'll probably find me building computers or fixing someone's wifi. Everything I make lives on my GitHub.
